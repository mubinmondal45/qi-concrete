Beyond our own shelf

25 cultivation novels and their donghua, at a glance

Urban cultivation fiction didn't appear out of nowhere — it grew out of the wider xianxia and wuxia web novel scene, much of which now has an animated (donghua) adaptation. Here's a quick reference to the genre's landmarks: the protagonist to know, and what each story is actually about. A handful haven't been animated yet and are marked "novel" rather than "donghua."

A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality donghua

Han Li — an unremarkable villager who survives the cultivation world not through talent but through caution, patience, and a willingness to walk away from fights he can't win.

Battle Through the Heavens donghua

Xiao Yan — a fallen genius who loses his cultivation overnight and rebuilds it with the help of a soul sealed inside a ring, climbing the Dou Qi ranks to reclaim his name.

Renegade Immortal (Xian Ni) donghua

Wang Lin — a farmer's son with no natural talent who claws his way up through sheer ruthlessness and caution, becoming one of the genre's most methodical protagonists.

Soul Land (Douluo Dalu) donghua

Tang San — a martial arts master reborn into a world of spirits and soul rings, who fuses his old life's hidden techniques with a new body to become a legendary Soul Master.

Soul Land II: The Peerless Tang Sect donghua

Huo Yuhao — a poor, talented orphan whose rare soul skill and quiet determination carry him from an academy underdog to a pillar of his generation.

Martial Universe (Wu Dong Qian Kun) donghua

Lin Dong — cast out of his clan for weak talent, he trains in isolation with an ancient bracelet spirit and returns to humiliate the family that discarded him.

Swallowed Star donghua

Luo Feng — thrust into a world ravaged by monsters after a meteorite strikes Earth, he trains obsessively to protect his family in a setting closer to survival sci-fi than classic xianxia.

Perfect World donghua

Shi Hao — born in a remote, spiritually "dead" village, he sets out to prove that origin doesn't decide destiny in a setting built on ancient, near-mythological scale.

Jade Dynasty (Zhu Xian) donghua

Zhang Xiaofan — the sole survivor of his village's massacre, torn between the righteous sect that raised him and a darker path that keeps finding him.

A Will Eternal donghua

Bai Xiaochun — a self-serving, thoroughly unheroic cultivator whose comedic scheming somehow keeps landing him legendary opportunities he never asked for.

Tales of Herding Gods (Mu Shen Ji) donghua

Qin Mu — raised in isolation on sacred instruments and forbidden knowledge, he journeys into a fractured empire where gods, demons, and old wars are far from settled.

Stellar Transformations (Xing Chen Bian) donghua

Qin Yu — a young cultivator whose path stretches from a small mountain sect all the way to galaxy-spanning, planet-cracking power.

Ruthless Valiant donghua

Li Xingyun — investigating a clan's hidden secrets, his search for answers pulls him into the political fallout of a rebellion that nearly toppled an empire.

Against the Gods donghua

Yun Che — flung into an unfamiliar world on his wedding day, he claws his way from a crippled cultivation base to challenging emperors and gods alike.

The Daily Life of the Immortal King donghua

Wang Ling — an immensely powerful immortal who fakes amnesia to live a quiet, ordinary school life, mostly failing to stay unnoticed.

Apotheosis (Feng Qi Tianxia) donghua

Luo Zhen — betrayed and left for dead by his own sect, he's given a second chance at a legendary academy where old enemies don't recognize him.

Spirit Sword Sovereign donghua

Chu Chen — a modern-day exorcist who inherits a legendary sword spirit and is pulled between his ordinary school life and a hidden war between cultivators.

Yuan Zun (Origin) donghua

Zhou Yuan — stripped of his genius reputation and mocked as untalented, he starts over from the bottom of a sect built on a rare, dangerous energy source.

The Great Ruler (Emperor's Domination) donghua

Qin Chen — reduced to nothing after a catastrophic setback, he clings to survival long enough to plot a comeback across worlds and generations.

I Shall Seal the Heavens novel

Meng Hao — a failed scholar kidnapped into a sect of immortal cultivators, whose stubbornness and cunning carry him further than any of his more talented rivals.

Desolate Era novel

Ji Ning — reincarnated with memories of a past life's failed cultivation attempt, he sets out to correct old mistakes across an unimaginably vast, ancient universe.

Coiling Dragon novel

Linley Baruch — heir to a once-legendary but now-decrepit warrior clan, he trains to restore his family's fallen glory while carrying a rare, misunderstood bloodline.

Throne of Seal donghua

A politically dense xianxia epic built around sealed techniques, shifting sect alliances, and the long fallout of an ancient war between immortal factions.

Sovereign of the Three Realms donghua

A once-mighty ruler is stripped of his throne and forced to rebuild his standing from the very bottom, one contested realm at a time.

Full-Time Magister donghua

Mo Fan — transported into a world where magic runs on academy rankings and elemental affinities, he goes from the weakest student in his class to one of its most feared.
